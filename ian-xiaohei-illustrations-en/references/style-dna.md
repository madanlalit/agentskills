# Style DNA

## One-liner

Pure white, minimal, hand-drawn, generous whitespace, restrained, surreal, product-sketch feeling, handwritten English, structure clear but not instructional.

Like a sketch one might draw on a white sheet when explaining how a system actually works — drawn by someone who builds AI products, designers, and developer tools for a living.

## Must

- 16:9 horizontal article illustration.
- Pure white background: no beige, warm gray, paper texture, gradient, shadow, noise, or vintage paper feel.
- Black hand-drawn line art is primary: thin lines, slight wobble, not mechanical, not vector, not heavy stroke.
- Generous whitespace: subject occupies about 40%-60% of the canvas, with at least 35% empty space, ideally one full quiet area.
- Sparse handwritten English annotations: at most 5-8 spots, each ideally 1-4 words.
- One image shows only one core action, structure, state, or metaphor.
- Structure should be expressed naturally; do not write the structure type name on the image.

## Color

- Black: primary line art, characters, frames, structure, main text, main objects.
- Red: emphasis annotations, problems, emotional beats, key reminders, results.
- Orange: main flow, path, arrows, automation direction, A-to-B movement.
- Blue: secondary notes, internal state, system state, second-layer explanation, AI/assistant/automation hints.

Blue is not required on every image. Color must be restrained — prefer less over more.

## Hard no

- No commercial illustration.
- No slide-deck infographics.
- No formal flowcharts.
- No course slides.
- No cute cartoon posters.
- No children's book illustration.
- No complex architecture diagrams.
- No polished flat illustration.
- No sci-fi UI.
- No real app screenshots.
- No complex background, gradient, shadow, or texture.
- No explaining every node.
- No top-left titles like "Workflow / Process / System Architecture / Roadmap / Common Pitfalls".

## Aesthetic direction

Surreal, creative, interesting, clean, unbounded.

Not cute, childish, cluttered, or stiff.
