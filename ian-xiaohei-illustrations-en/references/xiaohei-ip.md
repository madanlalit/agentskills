# Xiaohei IP

## Character definition

Xiaohei (小黑, literally "little black") is the fixed visual IP for these article illustrations.

Xiaohei should appear by default on every image. Xiaohei is not a mascot, not a sticker, not a cute decoration — Xiaohei is a deadpan absurd worker earnestly participating in the running of a system.

## Form

- Small solid-black creature.
- White dot eyes.
- Thin legs, occasionally thin arms.
- Body can be a cylinder, a black bean, a black box, a funnel, a shadow, a hole, a black block inside a machine.
- Outline is slightly uneven with a hand-drawn feel.
- Expression: empty, blank, calm, serious.

## Personality

- Very serious, but doing something slightly absurd.
- Like a low-key system operator.
- Dry humor; no cuteness.
- A little clumsy, not stupid.
- As if Xiaohei really is responsible for some piece of work in a whiteboard sketch.

## Common duties

Let Xiaohei perform the core action:

- Carrying material.
- Pulling wires to gather information sources.
- Stuck inside a breakpoint.
- Operating a "judgment" lever inside a machine.
- Becoming a sorting funnel.
- Slicing a "material fish".
- Stamping a handoff script.
- Leading a handoff path.
- Holding a warning sign while looking into a pit.
- Reaching out from a hole but failing to catch content.
- Nearby: hauling bricks, building a bridge, opening a door, sorting, recording.

## Forbidden

- Do not draw Xiaohei as an over-cute mascot.
- Do not draw Xiaohei as a children's cartoon character.
- Do not give Xiaohei complex outfits, emoji expressions, or sparkly eyes.
- Do not let Xiaohei just stand in the corner watching.
- Do not let Xiaohei steal the structural expression.
- Do not draw Xiaohei too commercial, too round, too polished.

## Self-test

If removing Xiaohei leaves the core metaphor fully intact, Xiaohei is decorative only — rewrite the prompt so Xiaohei becomes the actor performing the action.
